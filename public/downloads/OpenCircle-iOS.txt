OpenCircle for iPhone & iPad
============================

Install as a Home Screen app (full-screen, no Safari chrome):

1. Open Safari (required on iOS)
2. Go to https://opencircle-lol-creative.vercel.app
3. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
4. Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Name it OpenCircle → Add

Open the icon from your Home Screen — it runs like a native app
with the same layout and features as the website.

Tip: Allow notifications when prompted for the best experience.
